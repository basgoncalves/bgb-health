Physical assessment

See how you really move.

Using phone video and simple at-home tests, we measure what usually only a lab can see: how your joints move, where you're strong, and where load builds up.

  • Movement screen for squat, lunge, hinge and balance
  • Running or sport-technique video analysis
  • Strength and mobility benchmarks to track over time
  • A training plan that fits your week and your goals

Nutrition assessment

Eat for the life you actually live.

We start from what you already eat, then adjust it to your training, your work schedule and your goals. No meal plans you'll drop after two weeks.

  • 3-day food and energy diary review
  • Protein, carbohydrate and recovery needs for your training
  • Meal timing around work, workouts and sleep
  • Simple, realistic swaps and a shopping guide
